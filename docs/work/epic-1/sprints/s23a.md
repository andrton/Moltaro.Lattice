# S23A · Evacuate nearly empty segments

**Track:** full, critical · **Needs:** S23 · **Owners:** E01, E03 ·
**Decision:** [Q66](../../open-questions.md#q66-may-segment-evacuation-start-without-a-sparse-occupied-region)

- **Goal.** Return a nearly empty native segment whose remaining large
  movable blocks occupy regions that cannot meet the ordinary S23 trigger.
  The work precedes budget enforcement in S24.
- **Contract.** No public API change. A separate automatic trigger may
  select a segment whose remaining live blocks can move into compatible
  capacity already available outside it. The ordinary region trigger
  remains in force. Use the existing maintenance admission, atomic
  address publication and epoch-safe reclamation. Preserve the
  standing reserve of one segment. The capacity of retired copies still
  held by readers plus the proposed destination must fit in 64 KiB
  of working headroom, plus metadata. Preserve the existing native
  memory limit and carry the actual capacity to S24.
- **To read.** [Q17](../../../decisions/allocation-and-compaction.md),
  [arena reclamation and relocation](../../../design/arena-protocol.md#reclamation-and-relocation),
  [the S23 owner decision](s23.md#s23-closure), and the
  [Q66 allocator probe](s23.md#q66-segment-evacuation-probe). The probe
  returned 4,194,304 native bytes with an existing external destination
  and zero bytes without one. Its synthetic owner and synchronous free
  establish the two geometries. The production tests run the ordinary
  worker and exercise reader protection.
- **Acceptance.** On master and child, use real 32 KiB and 64 KiB
  live slots that fail the strict region trigger. Run both destination
  layouts for each class. Use available space in another active
  segment: a compatible slot or unassigned free region for 32 KiB,
  and an unassigned free region for 64 KiB. Verify the actual address
  change, correct values and revisions for concurrent reads, retained
  views and export, and return of an actual 4 MiB segment after the
  last reader leaves. Verify the original reserve and live bytes by
  an independent ledger. With the same source and no compatible
  external destination, verify zero relocation allocations, zero
  returns and preserved reads and reserve across repeated attempts.
  Exercise a held reader across multiple eligible sources and
  measure the relocation headroom. Check that a source with an
  immovable secondary index node is ineligible for evacuation and
  index lookups still work after movement of data they reference.
  Check visible progress over bounded steps, at most one block
  per turn, and the usual write admission, fault and close boundaries.
- **Measured.** On matching master and child layouts with the trigger
  enabled and disabled, report actual moves and returns, start,
  peak and final native bytes, standing reserve and relocation
  bytes, live data, full turn time from selection through reclamation
  against the 1 ms budget, and concurrent reader p99. Include first
  and steady turns and both destination layouts. Keep independent
  tests for both layouts.

## Study and internal contract

The allocator study examined the BSD-licensed sources of [memcached 1.6.39](https://github.com/memcached/memcached/blob/62b3447380d3fd547e432d1128d94cd12f2e6852/slabs_mover.c#L73-L169),
[V8 12.4.254.21](https://github.com/v8/v8/blob/8ab91836885699b9ef686c3a30df4778501ef7d1/src/heap/mark-compact.cc#L476-L617),
and [Valkey 8.1.0](https://github.com/valkey-io/valkey/blob/67c8683792fc9ab7e295f833478eca180c5e4691/src/defrag.c).
Memcached requires an existing slot outside its victim page and holds replaced
items while references remain. Its per-slot map, cache eviction and retry policy
serve a different lifetime and capacity model. V8 selects by live bytes in the
unit it can return and caps evacuation work. Its collection phase can sort pages,
grow to-space and fix pointers in bulk. Valkey resumes owner-aware work under a
time budget. Its slab selection and immediate free do not supply this arena's
segment accounting or reader lifetime. The design takes these principles and uses
the existing Lattice mover and retirement protocol.

`AutomaticMaintenance` keeps the ordinary below-half region rule and searches
for an assigned same-class vacancy in an earlier segment on that path. The new
path selects a live 32-KiB or 64-KiB slot from a segment with at most one sixteenth
of its capacity in published slot bytes, including headers and class slack.
Prepared and retired slots still occupy capacity and cannot supply a free slot.
An allocated secondary-index node prevents selection by the new rule. The cutoff
is inclusive at 256 KiB in a 4-MiB segment, bounding the live capacity considered
to at most one sixteenth of the native return. It is a conservative initial choice.

`Arena` may offer an assigned compatible slot or an unassigned region in another
active segment. The same Writes admission revalidates ownership, charge, order and
space before the existing mover claims it. Claim an unassigned region only after
retirement capacity is reserved, through the arena's ordinary free-to-class list
transition. The original reserve stays in place and the move uses no new native
segment. Use a destination with higher published slot charge, or with a lower
segment number at equal charge. Apply the rank in either numerical direction.
A vacant slot can take an individual block even when other survivors have no
destination. Each turn retains its selection and reclamation limits and publishes
at most one move. The old slots and the next copy together charge at most 64 KiB.

At stable logical load, the sum of ordinary movable slot bytes times their
segment numbers falls on each ordinary move. The new rule moves distinct classes
and preserves that sum. For a new-rule move of size `p`, source charge `x` and
destination charge `y`, the sum of squared segment charges rises by
`2p(y - x + p)` because `y >= x`. Reclaiming a retired copy changes neither measure.
Together these give finite progress over both rules, including partial capacity.

Derive threshold checks from 256 KiB and the adjacent representable charges
at 16-byte intervals using a mixed-class source. Verify rank ties, both numerical
directions and an intervening write that consumes the selected capacity. Exercise
32 + 32 KiB exactly at headroom and refusal of 32 + 64 KiB while an old copy is
held. Time a real 64-KiB leaf and a same-class owner with many actual children,
separately under concurrent reads. Pair the added write-side accounting against
the parent on an identical ordinary write workload and record allocations.

## S23E retirement queue upkeep on a running instance

A write can empty a high-water retirement queue immediately after publishing.
An allocation refusal while reducing its empty backing faulted a master
after returning the committed result. A child applied the same triggering
forwarded write and returned its receipt, then lost the link and refused its
next forwarded write. The fault was reproduced on `677d1281` with public
writes on both roles. An independent no-transport control on the `887303f9`
source observed the empty, unreserved queue with a capacity of 16,384 slots,
then checked the identical strict-close outcome and final native return.

**Acceptance.** For a running node, an allocation refusal solely from reducing
the empty retirement queue leaves the reclaimed blocks free and the queue
usable. The triggering committed and forwarded results and a subsequent write
remain observable on both roles. Final close still joins admitted operations
and protected readers, preserves the first cleanup error and returns the arena.

The independent tests-first review accepted
`93b34671ba3ecb6e1b4f216a6457bdd1d6dd65be`. The exact commit passed build,
format and six focused controls. The two intended regression rows remained
skipped until implementation. The earlier forced run reproduced both failures
without those skips.

The implementation at `782424ae83007ba713421356266349ca2ad3aed1` shares
optional trim handling across the synchronous drain and the bounded worker.
The bounded worker still charges one unit for an attempted trim. Strict
close reports its original error and continues with native cleanup.

The independent review approved the ordinary, memory recovery and final
close paths. All 51 related tests passed in each of Debug and Release. The
coordinator's full Windows x64 Release run passed 4,683 tests, with 129
expected skips, format and all 18 samples. An independent three-case
Release comparison held two protected readers. The refused ordinary trim
kept only the empty managed backing, the next bounded pass spent one unit
reducing it to zero, and a subsequent write reached the next position.
Close returned every native segment. The strict close case kept both
callers waiting for the readers and returned the same original exception
after the native return. Raw checks stay outside Git.

## S23F child scratch after publishing or returning a batch

A child discards oversized preparation scratch after naming more than
1,024 keys. Replacing it at that point could allocate after publication or
rollback. The controlled cases returned an allocation error for a coherent
committed batch, replaced the original preparation error during rollback,
and tried to return the same native roots twice after a real arena refusal.
An independent mixed replace, delete and insert batch also checked the
transferred unique-index owners and shared text references.

**Acceptance.** Releasing oversized scratch leaves the published outcome
or original preparation error intact. Native refusal returns prepared
effects once per attempt and keeps its existing retry and refusal result.
The next preparation obtains new scratch under the writes lock before
acquiring native storage. A refusal there leaves the last position and
ownership intact. The receiver can prepare the same input on a later
invocation. The live follower ends the connection when application of a
batch actually fails. Small batches keep room under the existing rule.

The independent tests-first review accepted
`4197b7e9e27c46ed3592b4e0f7532160685057fa`. Its exact parent
`81396918cd3ef7cfc9e5c3e5ad840dffbab80b6a` produced the three expected
red cases and two positive controls when forced. With normal skips restored,
the tests-first solution builds, format passes, and the three S23D rollback
tests pass.

The source review approved `313a24a733fb9a9cfbf5ea9ee09c7fce57714c66`.
The five scratch cases and the three full-root rollback cases passed in
Debug and Release. The associated load, transaction, following and protection
cases passed in both configurations.

An independent 1,025-member mixed-batch control verified committed payloads,
revisions and transferred unique owners, the original preparation error, and
unchanged position, index, text and native state after a refused recreation.
Retry on the same receiver advanced one position. After the ordinary
quiescent drain, the ledger showed each native segment returned once at close.
Both independent cases passed on Windows x64 in Debug and Release. The
fixture SHA-256 is
`f8961758599f1e0b805eb50cf219aa80d8faa20097869afea05da3c6e28099ba`.
Raw results stay outside Git.
